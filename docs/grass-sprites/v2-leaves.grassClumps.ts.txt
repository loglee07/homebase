// Hand-drawn grass clusters for the cobblestone edge. Each sprite is five rows of pixels, drawn the way grass grows out of the lawn
// onto the outermost stones: the dark roots stand on the lawn (rows 0-1, above the stone's first pixel row, row 2), and the thin
// leaves run down over the stone to a lit tip, two to four per cluster, uneven in length and leaning a pixel here and there.
// d = dark root, m = mid leaf, l = lit tip, . = nothing. Same palette as the lawn (FRINGE in paving.ts). Placement, mirroring and
// overlap depth are chosen in paving.ts.
